**What this sweep does and does not cover.** It refreshes the two things that are purely factual and go stale fastest: the org repository inventory and the App Center status badges. It does **not** touch the build matrices, the NASA asset mapping, or the use cases — those involve judgement and stay human-edited.

**Two standing rules for anyone reading a diff here.** A website is evidence about *publishing*, never about *operations* — do not infer from a stale page that a hub or service has stopped. And *retired is not excluded*: a service moving to No Longer Active belongs in the reactivation table, not the bin.
